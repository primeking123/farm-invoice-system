Our Mission

Bridging Agriculture & Financial Access

Founded with a clear purpose: turning commercial farming operations across East Africa into verifiable financial proof.

The Story Behind June Harvest

Commercial farmers and farming cooperatives produce substantial value every season, yet remain locked out of formal agricultural credit, bank financing, and fair market contracts because their activities exist only in disorganized paper notes or memory.

June Harvest changes that. We built a minimal, high-visibility platform that captures farming activities directly in the field, structures them into quarterly seasons, and compiles verified financial statements that banks, buyers, and agricultural lenders can rely on.

Capture Rapidly

Generate digital, standardized invoices in under two minutes without requiring complex accounting knowledge.

Store Reliably

Keep your season records resilient and accessible even in rural areas with intermittent connectivity using cache storage.

Trace Verifiably

Every transaction carries a unique sequential ID, date stamp, customer contact, and payment status for bank appraisal.
