Our Story

Commercial farmers across Uganda struggle to keep reliable records, even when using database applications.

June Harvest is a SaaS platform designed to change that. We give farmers a simple, reliable way to capture, store, and trace every transaction in one place.

How We Help

Our goal is to help farmers build proof of their business so they can access loans from banks with confidence.

  • Capture — Create digital invoices in under two minutes.
  • Store — Keep data safe in the cloud, accessible on any device, even offline.
  • Trace — Every transaction gets a unique ID, date, and buyer contact.

Our Vision

Making credible financial records for commercial farmers. We aim to help organize work properly, maintain reliable records, and increase access to bank loans.
