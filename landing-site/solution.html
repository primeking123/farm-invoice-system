The Solution

Built for Farm Commerce & Financial Access

June Harvest is not another complicated accounting tool. It is a purpose-built trust system that captures farm operations through the seasons and turns them into credible financial records.

Farming Cooperatives

Challenge: Disorganized paper records, unstructured harvest photos, and no standardized verification for cooperative member transactions.

Solution: Unified seasonal registers, member trade consolidation, and formal invoice records with audit logs.

Agricultural Buyers

Challenge: Zero visibility into verified farm produce volumes, leading to frequent disputes over quantities, weights, and due payments.

Solution: Transparent digital invoices with unit pricing, payment terms, and clear delivery tracking.

Commercial Farmers

Challenge: Lost receipts, lack of structured seasonal margins, and inability to prove trading volume to access working capital.

Solution: Fast invoice generation in under 2 minutes, tracked customer balances, and permanent cloud-safe records.

Banks & Ag-Lenders

Challenge: Absence of reliable, historical trading statements to assess creditworthiness and underwrite farm loans.

Solution: Formal seasonal financial statements, revenue aging schedules, and exportable reports in PDF and Excel format.

Every harvest recorded. Every season verified.

June Harvest bridges the gap so your farm's hard work becomes measurable proof for financial growth.
