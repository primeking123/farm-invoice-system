Our Style of Working

We help farmers create proof of their business transactions to increase their chances of getting loans from banks. We achieve this using three main techniques.

01

Capture

Create digital invoices within less than two minutes, making it easier for farmers to record their transactions.

02

Store

Securely store farmer data in the cloud. Farmers can access records on any device, even offline, and synchronize when online.

03

Trace

Every transaction receives a unique ID, date and buyer contact, making records easier to verify and trace.

Our Vision

Making Credible Financial Records for Commercial Farmers

To create a farmer-friendly platform that does more than just keep records. We aim to help farmers organize their work properly, maintain reliable financial records and increase their chances of accessing loans from banks.
