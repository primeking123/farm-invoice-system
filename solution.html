Solution

June Harvest is not another accounting tool. It is a trust system for agricultural invoices.

Farmer Cooperatives

Unstructured invoice photos, no verification process,no official record list

Agricultural Buyers

No visibility of real farmer produce volumes,disputes over quantity and payment

Smallholder Farmers

Loss of paper receipts, no proof of trading activity, no invoice status feedback

Potential Lenders(future)

No verifiable trading history to demonstrate activity. June Harvest does not promise loan approval.

June Harvest is how trust grows from every verified transaction.
